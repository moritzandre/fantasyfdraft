Strategy Brief · S2 · Slot 1 — the pick-1 decision tree

tick the first condition that is TRUE, top to bottom — the MC numbers are the printed form of the same engine the app runs · CoW = cost of waiting

You pick at 1, then 24 (gap 23 — LONG side, κ 1.3: weight CoW up), then 25 (gap 1).

On the clock at pick 1 — check in order

A · Last elite RB

A Tier-A RB is still on the board at pick 1
MC @ pick 1: E[Tier-A RBs left] = 4.0 · P(≥1) = 100%
CoW: he survives to pick 24 with P = <1% — waiting risks the full A→B cliff (18 pts). (1 − survival) × cliff ≈ the whole cliff.
→ TAKE HIM. The last elite RB does not live through a 23-pick wait.
— else ↓ —

B · Best deep-tier WR

No Tier-A RB left — or a Tier-A WR clearly outranks the best RB
MC @ pick 1: E[Tier-A WRs left] = 6.0 · P(≥2) = 100% · Tier B behind is 16.0 deep
CoW: the ELITE WR is the scarce asset — Tier A holds to pick 24 with only P = 2%. The B-tier depth behind him is exactly why your SECOND WR can wait: E[best WR at 24] barely drops.
→ Take the best Tier-A WR now; plan RB/RB or RB/TE at picks 24 and 25.
— else ↓ —

C · Elite TE

RB and WR Tier A both gone (or only tied, sub-45-rec options left)
MC @ pick 1: TE Tier A P(≥1) = 100% · holds to 24 with P = 100%
CoW: if the hold probability to pick 24 is high, the elite TE WAITS — CoW ≈ 0 and taking him now burns the RB/WR cliff instead. Take him at 1 only when the hold drops under ~60% (cliff to next TE tier: 11 pts — the 2-FLEX elite-TE edge).
→ Default: pass, and let branch A or B decide — the tree exists for the day the room breaks it.

CoW, one line: CoW(i) = (1 − survival to your next pick) × (his value now − the best you'd expect at that position when pick 24 comes back around).
